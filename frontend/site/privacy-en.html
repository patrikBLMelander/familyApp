KidQuest is an app where children tick off daily chores and collect food for a pet that grows. Parents set up the chores and manage the family and the children's pocket money.

This page describes what data the app processes and why. It is written to be readable — if anything is unclear, I would like to hear about it.

Who is responsible

The data controller is:

Patrik Melander, Sweden
Email: support@kidquest.se

What data is processed

DataAbout whomWhy
Name Adults and children So family members recognise each other in the app. A first name is enough.
Email address Adults only Signing in and resetting the password. Children have no email address in the app.
Password Adults only Signing in. Never stored in plain text, only as a cryptographic hash.
Device key Everyone Keeps the device signed in. Stored encrypted on the phone and not included in backups.
Chores and completions Children The feature itself: what needs doing and what has been done.
Points and pets Children The reward system.
Pocket money Children Balance, savings goals and expenses that the parent records. This is bookkeeping of numbers, not real money or a bank account.
Language and currency Adults and children / the family Which language the app is shown in, if you have chosen one, and which currency the family's pocket money is shown in.
Purchase history The family If the family has a subscription: that it is valid.

What the app does not do

  • No location, no contacts, no photos.
  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking service. The app does not measure how you use it.
  • No selling or renting of data to anyone.

About the camera

The app asks for camera access for one reason only: to read the QR code that links a child's phone to the family. The image never leaves the phone and nothing is saved. The code is read directly in the app.

Children's data

The parent creates the child's account and decides which name is used. The child never signs up on their own and gives no email address. The data about a child is the name, the chores, the points, the pet and the pocket money — nothing else.

If you would rather use a nickname instead of the child's real name, the app works exactly the same.

Who can see the data

A family's data is visible within the family. No other family can see it. In addition, the following providers are used to run the service:

ProviderWhat
RailwayServers and the database where the data is stored.
ResendSends emails when a password is reset.
RevenueCatHandles subscription status for families who pay.
Apple and GoogleDistribute the app and take payment for purchases. They are the seller to the customer; payment details never pass through KidQuest.

How long data is kept

For as long as the family uses the app. If you delete the account, everything is removed — see Delete account (in Swedish). Accounting records for completed purchases may need to be kept longer because the law requires it, but they concern the payment, not the family's content.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as a hash and cannot be read, not even by me.
  • The device key is stored encrypted on the phone with a key that never leaves the device, and it is not included in cloud backups.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to know what data is processed about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to receive it in a readable format and to object to the processing.

Get in touch at support@kidquest.se and I will help you. If you are not happy with how a request is handled, you have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If the change matters for how data is processed, families are informed in the app.
